BAudio privacy policy

Effective 15 November 2026

BAudio ("BAudio - Music & Radio Player") is a music and internet-radio player for Android made by Hotpatch Audio. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short, BAudio does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and BAudio has no servers of its own.

Music on your phone

To play your music, BAudio asks for permission to read the audio files on your phone. It reads their titles, artists, albums and cover art to build your library. Your music and this information stay on your phone and are never uploaded anywhere.

Internet radio

The radio directory comes from Radio Browser, a free community database of radio stations.

  • When you browse or search for stations, BAudio sends your search words to Radio Browser's servers.
  • When you play a station from the directory, BAudio tells Radio Browser which station was played. This is how the directory counts popular stations.
  • The radio stream and the station's logo come directly from that station's own server.

As with any internet request, these servers can see your device's IP address. BAudio sends nothing that identifies you. Radio Browser and each station have their own privacy practices.

BAudio Pro purchase

BAudio Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. BAudio never sees your payment details. It only receives confirmation from Google Play that Pro has been bought, so the Pro features can be unlocked. See Google's privacy policy.

Information stored on your phone

BAudio keeps its settings, playlists, favourites and play queue on your phone so they are there next time you open the app. Uninstalling BAudio deletes them.

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include this app data in your device backup to your own Google account, as it does for most apps. You control this in your phone's settings.

Permissions

  • Music and audio: to find and play the music on your phone.
  • Notifications: to show playback controls in the notification and on the lock screen.
  • Internet: for radio streams, the station directory and station logos.
  • Foreground service and wake lock: to keep music playing with the screen off.

Children

BAudio is a general music player. It is not designed for children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date.
